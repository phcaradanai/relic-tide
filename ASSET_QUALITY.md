## Credits and source handling

**This composition pass used no additional PixelLab generations or DepthFlow credits.** DA-V2 ran locally through the configured project pipeline. One environment sheet was created with the built-in imagegen tool in edit mode, using the existing dungeon painting as its material reference. The exact returned PNG, prompt, origin and SHA-256 manifest are retained under `assets/generated/environment/sluice-set-v2.*`.

Historical PixelLab production used **82 generations**, taking the account from **1,994 to 1,912 of 2,000**. The grip/pickup extension used **124 more**, taking **1,912→1,788**. These are the last verified allowances from those runs, not a fresh account balance. The provider's USD credit balance was 0; subscription generations are not a claimed dollar charge. Palette variants and native grip/rear-pose repairs were local authoring.

The v2 private character export preserves 1,352 PNGs; the original 552-PNG ZIP remains unchanged. One sampled animation response included a leading reference, while the managed ZIP explicitly lists twelve animation frames separately from the rotation references. Packing follows provider metadata rather than dropping a guessed frame. Runtime atlases are 4050×2790, under the 4096 limit. Private IDs, URLs and credentials are not exported.

Historical DepthFlow requests are preserved as studies. Two earlier Dolly clips cost twenty credits each (**600→580→560**) and barely moved. The later individually approved Workshop Orbit cost twenty credits (**560→540**), produced an 8.0667s, 400×380, 30fps clip without a visible watermark, and had an actual five-second repeating camera cycle. Its movement was measurable, but the user rejected the result when applied to the playable map. The Orbit resources and old mask packer are excluded from the current web export. No further render was submitted, and billing/renewal settings were not changed.
